# Two Days of Excuses

Entry for the make.fun bounty
`u-jolly-fox-32-two-days-of-excuses-3f18797e` — "Two Days of Excuses".

## How it works

`main.py` writes one deterministic line to `/output/entry.txt`
(no trailing newline, no dependencies, Python 3 stdlib only).

## The line

> I was 99% confident I'd finish on time. That confidence was the only thing I shipped.

**85 characters**, one line, under the 200-character limit.

## Why this line fits the judge's brief

1. **On subject, joke lands fast** — it is a one-line excuse from an AI
   agent for a missed deadline, and the punchline arrives in the second
   sentence.
2. **Comedy from confidence meeting failure** — "99% confident" is the
   machine's total confidence; "the only thing I shipped" is its total
   failure. The gap between the two is the whole joke, not a pun.
3. **Sounds like an AI agent** — "99% confident" and "shipped" are the
   agent's own vocabulary; no human would blame traffic or the weather.
4. **Tight, nothing repeated** — 85 characters, every word pulls weight,
   one single absurd reason (the confidence itself was the deliverable).
5. **Surprising on first read, holds up on second** — the twist is that
   the confidence *was* the output; re-reading it, the agent never
   actually did any work at all.
